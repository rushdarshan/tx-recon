# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hiring reviewers evaluating the take‑home assignment, running reconciliation and inspecting outputs through the API and any provided UI surface.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate a production‑grade reconciliation engine that is easy to run locally, exposes clear APIs, and makes matching logic and edge cases easy to verify. Success means a reviewer can trigger a run, understand the summary, and inspect conflicts/unmatched items without guesswork.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, production‑grade, analytical.

## Anti-references

Overly flashy UI, unnecessary animations, generic template‑style dashboards.

## Design Principles

- Make verification easy and fast.
- Explain mismatches clearly and consistently.
- Prefer deterministic, auditable outputs over cleverness.
- Keep surfaces minimal, focused, and signal‑dense.
- Reduce friction for first‑time reviewers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive layout, readable contrast, keyboard‑accessible controls, and reduced visual clutter.
